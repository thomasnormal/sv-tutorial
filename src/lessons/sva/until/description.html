An SRAM master must keep a request valid until the arbiter says it is done. The until family expresses "hold this condition while waiting for that one", and makes the choice between a permitted timeout and a real deadlock explicit:

<hold_condition> until <done_event>
<hold_condition> s_until <done_event>
<hold_condition> until_with <done_event>
  • p until q — weak: p holds at every tick until q becomes true. If q never occurs the property still holds (vacuously).
  • p s_until q — strong: same as above but q must eventually occur.
  • p until_with q — inclusive: p is also required to hold on the same cycle that q first becomes true.

Open hold_check.sv and complete two properties:

  • p_valid_holds: once valid goes high it must stay high until done arrives — and done must eventually arrive.
  • p_done_clean: from the moment valid is high, data_ok must hold on every cycle including the cycle that done fires.

This builds on sva/always-eventually: s_until combines a hold condition with the eventual progress guarantee, while until_with makes the completion cycle inclusive. These are the same request/response obligations an SRAM scoreboard checks around each transaction.

The nexttime operator is the single-step companion: nexttime p requires p to hold at the very next clock tick. s_nexttime p is the strong variant (fails if there is no next tick). These combine naturally with until to build recursive temporal patterns.
